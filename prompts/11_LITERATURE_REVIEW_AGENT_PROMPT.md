# Literature Review Agent Prompt

Conduct a focused, verifiable literature review for EvoOptLab.

## Topics
- multi-objective evolutionary optimization;
- NSGA-II and successor methods;
- Differential Evolution and multi-objective variants;
- Particle Swarm Optimization variants;
- Bayesian optimization and HPO;
- benchmark design and fair algorithm comparison;
- statistical testing for multiple optimization algorithms;
- compute-aware and multi-objective HPO.

## Method
1. Search primary research papers, reputable surveys, and official benchmark documentation.
2. Prioritize recent work while retaining seminal foundational papers.
3. Verify bibliographic metadata and DOI/official URL.
4. Read methods, experimental setup, limitations, and supplementary material for closest work.
5. Build `templates/literature_matrix.csv`.
6. Identify what has already been studied and what remains uncertain.
7. Propose a narrow research gap supported by evidence.

## Output
- search strategy and date;
- inclusion/exclusion criteria;
- literature matrix;
- thematic synthesis;
- comparison of closest studies;
- evidence-backed gap statement;
- revised RQs and contributions;
- complete BibTeX entries.

Never fabricate references. If a source cannot be verified, label it unverified and do not cite it as fact. Do not claim novelty merely because a search returned few results.
